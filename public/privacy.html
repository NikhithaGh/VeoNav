Privacy notice

Last updated October 18, 2026. AeroNav / VistaPath is a hackathon research prototype operated by the owner of Google Cloud project veonav.

Search, routing, and imagery

When you search, your query is sent through our server to Google Places. Planning sends the selected origin and destination to Google Routes. Loading the map or Street View connects your browser to Google Maps and may disclose your IP address and browser information to Google. We do not download or store Google's panorama images.

Device location

Device location is requested only after you choose a GPS feature and grant browser permission. Using your location as the route origin sends that chosen endpoint to Google. Continuous route matching happens on your device; WebSocket messages contain progress, speed, and a signed route session rather than raw continuous GPS fixes.

Cloud scene preparation

When automatic preparation is enabled, selected preview coordinates are sent to Pub/Sub, BigQuery, and Earth Engine. Cloud Storage holds generated metadata and preparation status. Gemini receives the selected preview intent and sampled terrain elevation, not your Google search text or panorama images. AI output is not verified driving advice.

Retention and security

Route sessions expire after one hour. Active preview metadata and spatial points expire after 24 hours; scheduled deletion and provider backup/time-travel retention can take longer. Daily quota counters are temporary. Full route instructions and search-result names are not persisted by AeroNav. Google Cloud operational logs may retain request timing, IP addresses, and sanitized error metadata under provider defaults. Browser-saved demo destinations remain until you remove them or clear local storage. Server credentials are kept in Secret Manager, not browser code.

Third-party policies

Google processes data under its own policies. See Google Privacy Policy, Google Cloud Privacy Notice, and our Terms of Use. Optional Google Fonts may also contact Google. This prototype is not intended for sensitive or confidential travel plans. Stop GPS or close the page to stop active tracking; do not use the app while operating a vehicle.

For a privacy concern, contact the person or organization that provided your AeroNav demo link. No user accounts or advertising profiles are created by this prototype.
